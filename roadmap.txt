Product Expansion Roadmap:
---
Authentication & Multi-Tenancy (Company IDs & Default Bases)
Using Supabase Auth, you can assign users to a company_id so everyone on a dispatch team shares company data.
User profiles will store their preferred default base location so it automatically pre-selects when they open the app.
---
Feature Tiers & Distribution Tools
Tiers (Basic, Pro, Enterprise): Lock advanced features (like automated Towbook exports or custom geofences) behind subscription roles.
Distribution: Desktop install builds (via Tauri or Electron), web app embedding, or managed team invites.
---
Integrations & Scale (Towbook API + 48 Geofences)
Towbook Export: A "Send to Towbook" button that pushes customer details, addresses, and calculated rates straight into an active Towbook dispatch ticket.
Expanded Geofencing: Scaling up your geofence engine to handle dozens of regional metro zones dynamically without slowing down calculations.
